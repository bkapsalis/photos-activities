Privacy Policy

Bill's Bay Area Fun Activities

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Bill's Bay Area Fun Activities ("the app") is a place to share photos of fun places to visit around the San Francisco Bay Area, and to talk about them. It runs on Android, iPhone and the web. It is made and run by Bill Kapsalis, an individual developer ("I" or "me" below).

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it.

The short version

  • The app collects only what it needs to work: your account, what you post, and basic usage and crash information.
  • There are no ads. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • Comments, chat messages and photos you post are public, together with the name you post under.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you posted, from the app at any time.

1. What the app collects

Your account

  • Google or Apple sign-in: your name and email address from that account. With Apple you can hide your email address; the app then gets a private relay address from Apple instead.
  • Email and password: your email address and the name you type. Your password is kept by Google's Firebase Authentication in scrambled (hashed) form. I never see it.
  • Guest: a random ID and a name like "Guest 7KQ2". No email address.
  • Every account gets a random user ID. Initials and a color for your avatar are made from your name.

What you post (public)

  • Comments on photos, and messages in the chat for each place, with the time you posted them.
  • Photos, if you upload them. Right now only the moderator can upload. A photo post includes the place, where the photo was taken, and details saved in the photo by the camera: when it was taken, the camera's make and model, and the altitude. Any other hidden data in the photo file is removed before upload.
  • Your posts show your account's name, initials and avatar color. Anyone can see posts, including people who are not signed in.
  • Likes: which photos you liked. Everyone sees how many likes a photo has, but only you, the photo's owner and the moderator can see who liked it.

Safety

  • Reports you send: what you reported, the reason, and any details you add. Only the moderator sees them.
  • The people you block, and the posts you reported and so no longer see. Only you can see these.
  • Notices the app sends you, for example when a post of yours was removed, and whether your account is banned.

Location

Only on Android and iPhone, only when you take a photo inside the app, and only if you allow it: the phone's location is saved with that photo so it can be shown on the map. The app does not use your location at any other time, and never in the background. You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Photos on your device and in Google Drive

The app reads only the photos you choose to upload. On the website you can choose photos from your Google Drive. The app can open only the files you pick, and only in your browser while you upload them.

Usage, crash and performance information

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens you open and what you do there, such as viewing or liking a photo, posting a comment or signing in. It also records your device type, operating system, app version, a random ID for the app on your device, and your approximate location (city or region), which Google works out from your IP address. It is not linked to your name, email address or account.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes or runs into an error, what went wrong, your device model, operating system, app version and your user ID, so the problem can be traced and fixed.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: how long screens and network requests take, with your device type, operating system, app version and country.
  • The app does not use your phone's advertising ID.

2. AI checks

  • To keep the app safe, new photos are checked by Google's Gemini AI, through Google Cloud's Vertex AI, for content that breaks the community rules. When the content filter is switched on, the text of new comments and chat messages is checked too. A post the AI flags is taken down and kept privately for the moderator to review.
  • The same AI can also recognize animals in photos. The app then posts a public comment about them, marked "AI Naturalist".
  • Google processes this content only to give the app its answer. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google does not use it to train its AI models.

3. How your information is used

  • To run the app: signing you in, and showing photos, comments, likes, chats and maps.
  • To keep it safe: AI checks, reports, blocks and bans.
  • To find and fix problems, and to see which parts of the app are used.
  • To answer you when you write to me.

It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with advertisers.

4. Who else handles your information

  • Google runs the app's servers and storage (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Firebase Hosting), the usage, crash and performance tools listed above, the AI checks (Vertex AI), the maps (Google Maps Platform), Google sign-in, and choosing photos from Google Drive. Your data is stored in the United States.
  • Apple, if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • If you tap Directions, the place's location is passed to the maps app on your device.
  • Everyone can see your public posts, as described above.
  • I may share information if the law requires it, or if it is needed to protect someone's safety.

Google and Apple handle this information under their own privacy commitments: Google Privacy Policy, Privacy and Security in Firebase, and Apple Privacy Policy. Your information is not shared with anyone else.

5. How long it is kept

  • Your account and your posts: until you delete them, or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, everything listed on the account deletion page is deleted within seconds. There are no backups, so it cannot be brought back.
  • Reports you sent stay with the moderator without your name. Reports about your posts keep a copy of the reported post and the name it was posted under, so abuse can still be dealt with. Every report is deleted automatically a year after the moderator closes it.
  • Server logs, which include your user ID: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Crash reports: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Usage statistics: kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months.
  • A guest account belongs to one device. Signing out or uninstalling the app ends it for good, and its posts stay up. Email me to have them removed.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete any of your comments, delete your account, and turn off location access in your phone's settings.
  • You can email me to ask for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it. I will answer within 30 days. Please write from the email address on your account, so I can tell the account is yours.
  • California residents: the app does not sell or share personal information for advertising. It does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, but it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not let anyone else do so for advertising.

7. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email me and I will delete it.

8. Security

Information travels between the app and Google's servers encrypted (HTTPS), and Google stores it encrypted. Access rules let only you, or the moderator, read your private information. No system is perfectly secure, but I work to keep yours safe.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
