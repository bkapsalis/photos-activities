Delete your account

Bill's Bay Area Fun Activities, by Bill Kapsalis

In the app

This works the same on Android, iPhone and the website, so you don't need to install the app to do it.

  1. Sign in, then tap your avatar (the circle with your initials) at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, at the bottom.
  4. Confirm it's you by signing in again with Google or Apple, or by typing your password. Guests skip this step.
  5. Tap Delete account.

Your account and everything in it are deleted within seconds, and you are signed out.

By email

Email bkapsalis@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". I will delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Guest accounts

A guest account exists only on the device where it was made. Delete it in the app before you sign out or uninstall. If it is already gone, email me the guest name (like "Guest 7KQ2") and what you posted, and I will remove your posts.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, with your name and email address.
  • Photos you posted, with their comments and likes.
  • Your comments on photos, and your chat messages.
  • Your likes. The like counts on those photos go down.
  • The people you blocked, and the posts you reported and hid.
  • Notices the app sent you, and any ban on your account.
  • The AI check results for your photos.
  • If you signed in with Apple on Android or iPhone, the app's access to your Apple Account is removed too. On the website, you can remove it yourself at account.apple.com, under Sign-In and Security, then Sign in with Apple.

There are no backups, so deleted information cannot be brought back.

What is kept

  • Reports you sent about other people's posts, without your name, so the moderator has a record of them.
  • Reports other people sent about your posts, with a copy of the reported post and the name it was posted under, so abuse can still be dealt with.
  • Both kinds of report are deleted automatically a year after the moderator closes them.
  • Server logs that mention your user ID, which are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Crash reports, which are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Usage statistics, which are not linked to your account and are kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months.

Delete only some posts

You can delete any of your comments yourself: tap the ⋮ menu on the comment, then Delete. To have a chat message removed, email me.
